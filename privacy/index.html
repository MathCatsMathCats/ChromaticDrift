Privacy

The short version: this website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. The app has no accounts of its own, and its banner ads are personalized only if you agree.

The app This website

The Chromatic Drift app

The app doesn't ask for your name, email address, photos or location, and we run no servers that receive data from it.

  • Ads: the free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob. Personalized ads are shown only with your consent. The "Remove ads" purchase removes them.
  • Progress: stored on your device and, if you're signed in, in your own iCloud account so it syncs between your devices. We can't see it.
  • Game Center: optional. If you're signed in, Apple handles your leaderboard score and achievements.

No cookies, no analytics, no tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking tools. Every page, picture, font and script comes from this website itself.
