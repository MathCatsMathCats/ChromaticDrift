You can't touch the colors – only their laws.

A color puzzle for iPhone and iPad. Balls grow, spread and clash across the field. You never move a single one: you set the rules for what happens when two colors meet, and try to turn the whole field one color.

How it works

You set the laws.
The colors do the rest.

  1. Balls grow and spread

    Every ball grows, charges up and reaches into a neighboring cell. An empty cell gets a new ball of the same color.

  2. +=?

    Every meeting has a rule

    When two colors meet, their rule decides: one wins and takes the cell, they stop and form a wall, they pop and both vanish, or it's a coin toss.

  3. One color wins the field

    Change the rules whenever you like, even before a level begins. Turn every ball one color to win. Fewer changes and a quicker time earn more stars.

  • WinsOne color takes the other's cell.
  • StopNothing happens. The two colors build a wall.
  • PopBoth balls vanish and leave room.
  • 50/50A coin toss decides who wins.
  • MixLater on, two colors make a third.

The campaign

A stranger.
A field of living colors.
The way home.

The stranger wakes in warm grass with no memory of lying down. Every level opens and closes with one line of their story, from the first humming field to the door that leads home. Each chapter brings something new to master.

  • Drifters that repaint balls
  • Strange field shapes
  • Mixing colors
  • Stone mazes
  • Laws carved in stone
  • Storms, quakes and rain

Good to know

Play your way

  • Stars for clever play

    Win a level for a star, and earn more by using few changes and finishing fast. Stuck? Skip a level and come back to it later.

  • Game Center

    A global leaderboard and achievements, if you're signed in to Game Center.

  • Sync with iCloud

    Your progress, scores and ball style follow you between your iPhone and iPad. No account to make.

  • Free to play

    The game is free with a small banner ad. A one-time purchase removes the ads for good.
